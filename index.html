MKRK privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Who we are

MKRK is an open-play pickleball app run by Perilous Orbit. This policy explains what MKRK stores, who can see it and how to delete it.

What we collect

Account: your email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash by our login provider).

Profile: first and last name, the 18+ confirmation, your self-rating, an optional DUPR ID and an optional photo.

Location: when you tap Check in, the phone shares its location once so we can confirm you are at the courts. We store the distance from the venue, not your location, and we never track you in the background.

Play: check-ins, your place in line, games, teams, results you report, your match rating and its history.

Feedback: whether a game was a good match and the ratings you give other players. These are private and only used to calculate ratings.

Preferences: players you mark as Partner, Like or Never.

Reports: reports you make about a player, and reports about you.

Notifications: a push token for your phone, so we can tell you when you are called to a court.

Who can see it

Other players at your session see your name, photo, rating, wins and losses on the leaderboard, and who is in their game.

Venue hosts see your name, email address, rating and check-ins at their venue, can change your match rating, and review reports.

Nobody else sees the ratings you give, your Never list, or who reported a player.

Service providers

Supabase hosts our database, login and file storage (United States). Resend sends our account emails. Apple delivers push notifications. They process data only to provide these services.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Profile. That deletes your login, profile, photo, preferences and push tokens. Games you played stay in other players' history as "Former player" so their results and ratings still add up.

Reports are kept so hosts can see patterns, with your name removed if you delete your account.

Age

MKRK is for people aged 18 and over.

Contact

Questions or requests: mkrk.feedback@liquidrnd.com. We will update this policy as the app changes and show the date at the top.
