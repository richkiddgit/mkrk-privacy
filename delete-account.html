Delete your MKRK account

MKRK, by Perilous Orbit

Delete it in the app

  1. Open MKRK and sign in.
  2. Tap Me at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Can't open the app?

Email mkrk.feedback@liquidrnd.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my MKRK account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your login, email address, name, profile photo, DUPR ID, Partner/Like/Never choices and push tokens.

What is kept

Games you played stay in other players' history as "Former player", so their results and ratings still add up. Reports you made or that were made about you are kept for venue safety, with your name removed. Nothing kept is linked to your email address.
